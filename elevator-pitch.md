# Zelko — Elevator Pitch

## The One-Liner
Zelko is the booking and management platform built specifically for barbershops — online booking, scheduling, client management, and team tools, all in one place.

## The 30-Second Pitch
Every barbershop runs on pen-and-paper, WhatsApp, or overpriced software that wasn't built for them. Zelko changes that. We give barbershops their own online booking page that clients can access 24/7 — no app download required, just a link. Behind the scenes, shop owners get a full management dashboard: day calendar, client profiles with notes and preferences, insights and revenue tracking, team management with commission splits, and a mobile app so they can run their shop from their pocket. Setup takes under 10 minutes. Starts at $29/month.

## The Problem
- Barbershops lose revenue to no-shows and phone tag scheduling
- Existing solutions (Booksy, Fresha, Square) are expensive, bloated, or force clients to download an app
- Most barbers still use paper books, Instagram DMs, or text messages to manage their schedule

## The Solution
- **For clients:** A clean booking page — pick your barber, pick your time, done. No app to download, just a link
- **For barbers:** A mobile app and web dashboard to manage their entire day — bookings, clients, insights, team
- **For shop owners:** Compensation tracking, barber performance, analytics, and everything they need to run the business

## What Makes Zelko Different
1. **Built for barbershops, not salons** — the language, the flow, the features are all barber-first
2. **No client app required** — customers book via a web link, not a forced app download (unlike Booksy)
3. **$29/month for everything** — not $50-100+ like competitors
4. **Mobile app for barbers** — manage your day from your back pocket
5. **Commission and rent tracking** — built-in, not an add-on

## The Numbers
- $29/mo Essentials (up to 3 barbers) / $49/mo Pro (unlimited)
- 14-day free trial, no credit card required
- Setup in under 10 minutes

## The Flow
1. Shop owner signs up at **zelko.ca** or downloads the app
2. Onboarding wizard: shop name → hours → add barbers → add services (2 minutes)
3. Gets a booking link: **app.zelko.ca/book/your-shop**
4. Shares the link with clients — QR code for the mirror, link for Instagram bio
5. Clients book online, barber sees it on their phone instantly
6. Shop owner tracks revenue, manages team, runs their business
